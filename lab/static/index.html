A SMALL INTERVENTION, A LARGE SHIFT
Change the signal.
Watch the story change.
A model already contains many ways to speak and describe itself. A neuron’s output adds a direction to its shared internal representation. Repeatedly pushing that direction can make a theme more likely at every step; generated words then reinforce the theme through the model’s own context.
h ← h + dose × rL × Wdown[:, n] / ‖Wdown[:, n]‖
One unit of normalized dose can shift the residual stream by an entire typical residual norm. A small number of edited neurons does not mean a small intervention. Large doses can also cause repetition, factual errors, or incoherence.
The normalization source matters: Neuronary uses its fixed calibration text; the Persona Library uses the prompt’s residual norms. Baseline comparisons, repeated prompts, and human review help separate a reproducible tendency from a striking one-off answer.
No weights are trained here. These are temporary inference hooks, removed after each run. A persona label is not evidence of consciousness, identity, or a uniquely localized personality.
